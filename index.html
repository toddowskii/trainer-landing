/ 05 — FAQ

How selective is the application?

Applications are reviewed manually within 48 hours. Approval depends on goal clarity, training history, budget fit, and your ability to commit to the logging requirements. Roughly one in three applicants is accepted.

Do I need gym access?

You need access to a barbell, dumbbells, and a cable station, or equivalent. Programming is built around your actual equipment, declared at intake.

What data do I need to submit?

Daily morning body weight, food logs, and weekly set videos from key lifts. Optional but useful: a DEXA scan or calibrated body-fat measurement at the start and end of each block.

Can I pause or leave early?

After the minimum term, you can pause or cancel with 14 days' notice. Within the minimum term, fees are non-refundable because assessment and programming are front-loaded.

Is this suitable with injuries or medical conditions?

Disclose them at intake. Programming adapts around known limitations, but it does not replace medical advice. Clearance from your physician may be required before acceptance.
